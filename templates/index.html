To use your own reagents, upload one or more files: .csv with a column smiles, or .sdf (e.g. exported from your chemical inventory). Several files are combined. Salts are split into their parts (e.g. amine hydrochloride → amine and HCl), protonated amines are neutralized, carboxylates are kept as anion and acid, and stereochemistry, isotope labels and duplicates are removed automatically.

SDF entries without a readable structure are looked up on PubChem by their CAS number or name. Only these names and CAS numbers are sent to PubChem, never your target molecule.

    Add your own reaction rule

    Write the forward reaction in MØD's GML format, including its conditions (constrainAdj, …): left = reactants, right = products. LAGER then creates the retrosynthetic rule used by the search. Please check it before saving. Saved rules are available to everyone using this LAGER installation.

    Names containing protection or deprotection are treated as cheap helper steps in the search, like the existing protection rules.

    0 Start from example reactions (optional)

    Paste or draw one to four examples of the same reaction as reaction SMILES (reactants>>products). LAGER balances them, maps the atoms (SynKit), finds the reaction centre and writes a draft forward rule into step 1. Several different examples make the rule better: atoms that differ between them are left out, and only conditions true for all of them are suggested.
